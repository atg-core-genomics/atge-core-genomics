About ATG

Where molecular precision meets clinical clarity.

Founded by genomics scientists and clinical specialists, ATG Core Genomics bridges the gap between cutting-edge sequencing technology and the everyday diagnostic needs of clinicians. We believe that genetic insight should be fast, interpretable, and immediately usable at the point of care.

Our mission: make genomic medicine accessible — not just to research labs, but to every clinician making decisions for their patients.
Our Core Principles
  • Clinical-grade accuracy in every report
  • Interpretations written for clinicians, not researchers
  • Rapid turnaround without compromising rigour
  • Direct genomicist support for complex cases
  • Secure, HIPAA-compliant data handling
  • Continuous pipeline validation and QC
